# Privacy

knave-mail runs only on your computer. It has no server, collects no analytics, and shares nothing with anyone.

It uses Gmail access to read, label, trash and send your mail. Your mail is cached on your computer, and your sign-in token is kept in your system keyring. Apart from remote images you choose to load, it connects only to Google.

knave-mail's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

To revoke access, remove knave-mail at [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). For questions, open an issue at <https://github.com/KarlHarnois/knave-mail/issues>.
